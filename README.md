Omnichannel Retail Sales & Inventory Analytics Dashboard
Overview

The Omnichannel Retail Sales & Inventory Analytics Dashboard is a Business Intelligence project designed to analyze retail sales, inventory, and profitability across multiple sales channels. Built using Power BI, SQL Server, and Python, the project transforms raw retail data into interactive dashboards that support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard provides comprehensive insights into sales performance, revenue, profit, inventory levels, customer demand, and regional trends. It helps businesses identify top-performing products, optimize inventory, evaluate promotional campaigns, and improve overall operational efficiency.

Objectives
Analyze sales performance across stores, regions, and product categories.
Monitor inventory levels to minimize stock shortages and overstocking.
Track revenue, profit, cost, and ROI through interactive KPIs.
Identify seasonal sales patterns and demand trends.
Measure the impact of discounts, holidays, and promotions on sales.
Support strategic business decisions with real-time visual analytics.
Key Features
Interactive Power BI dashboard
Sales trend analysis
Revenue and profit analysis
Inventory management insights
Demand forecasting

Product category analysis
KPI tracking and executive summary
Dynamic filters and slicers for easy exploration
Technologies Used

Business Value

This project demonstrates how retail organizations can leverage Business Intelligence to monitor performance, optimize inventory, improve profitability, and make informed business decisions using interactive dashboards and data analytics.
